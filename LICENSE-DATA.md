## `gigafida/data/` and `gigafida/results/`

Per-document numeral counts, document metadata (class, title, source, year, publisher, length),
derived features and results, computed by the author from Gigafida 2.1 through the CLARIN.SI KonText
concordancer.

These files are released under the **Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International
licence (CC BY-SA 4.0)**, <https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/>, the licence under which
CJVT publishes its own data derived from Gigafida (e.g. the frequency lists of words,
<http://hdl.handle.net/11356/1273>, and of collocations, <http://hdl.handle.net/11356/1415>).
If you use them, please also cite Gigafida:

> Krek, Simon, Špela Arhar Holdt, Tomaž Erjavec, Jaka Čibej, Andraž Repar, Polona Gantar, Nikola
> Ljubešić, Iztok Kosem and Kaja Dobrovoljc. 2020. Gigafida 2.0: The reference corpus of written
> standard Slovene. In *Proceedings of the Twelfth Language Resources and Evaluation Conference*,
> 3340–3345. Corpus: <http://hdl.handle.net/11356/2055>.

No corpus text is included.

## `ssj/results/`

The window-level feature matrix, scores, feature importances and per-register profiles computed
from the SSJ treebank (UD_Slovenian-SSJ, release 2.18). Because the treebank is licensed
**CC BY-SA 4.0** (<https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/>), these files are released
under the same licence. The treebank itself is not included; it is available from
<https://github.com/UniversalDependencies/UD_Slovenian-SSJ>.

## `ssj/synthetic/results/`

Scores on the synthetic corpus generated by `ssj/synthetic/make_synthetic.py`; CC BY 4.0.
